# Appendix D: Ethics Application

Ethical approval was requested through the Faculty of Science and Engineering ethics application form, submitted on 5 August 2026 (application ID 1485878566). The project involves no human participants and no personal data; it uses only public market data. The form is reproduced below. The applicant's name, email address, browser and IP address are blacked out, because this dissertation identifies its author by student ID only.

The project title and scope changed after the application was made. The final dissertation compares a PPO agent with a supervised baseline (DQN was not used) and studies how the evaluation can mislead (Section 1.3). The ethical position is unchanged: no human participants, public data only, and paper trading with no real money.
